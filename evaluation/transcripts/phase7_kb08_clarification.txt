  KB-08 v2 s1: If the first payment attempt fails, the order is not placed and the customer can retry the payment up to 3 more times within 30 minutes while the cart prices stay locked.
  KB-08 v2 s2: If money was debited for a failed payment, it is automatically reversed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, and no claim needs to be raised.
  KB-08 v2 s3: Once all 3 retries have also failed, the customer should switch to another payment method such as UPI, a different card, net banking or cash on delivery.

  KB-08 v2 s5: The first attempt plus 3 retries means at most 4 payment attempts in total.
  reason: Sentence 1 ('retry the payment up to 3 times') counts retries after the first attempt (up to 4 attempts in total under the strict reading), while sentence 3 ('After 3 failed attempts ... switch') counts attempts in total, so the two count rules contradicted each other. Found in the Phase 7 remediation verification (E08). Owner-approved interpretation: three additional retries after the initial failed payment attempt, giving a maximum of four total attempts.
